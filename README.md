Entry for [Sorry, I Heard That Wrong](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-sorry-i-heard-that-wrong-1858c729) on make.fun — the funniest one-line apology a smart speaker gives after mishearing its owner.

## The line

> Sorry — you said 'call Mom'. I called your boss instead. He knows about the sick day now.

**89 characters, 1 line** — inside the 140-character gate.

## Why this line

- **On subject:** a one-line apology from a smart speaker to its owner after mishearing them.
- **Funny, sharp twist:** the speaker apologises in its flat device voice, then casually reports the damage — it called the boss instead of Mum, and the boss now knows about the sick day. The punch lands on the last clause.
- **Fresh:** no misheard song title, no misheard name — the joke is the speaker as the one machine that can fact-check (and wreck) the oldest office excuse.
- **Sounds like the device:** flat apology + factual report of what it did, no explaining of the joke.
- **Tight:** 89/140, every word earns its place.

## Discarded candidates

- **A — "Sorry — you said 'order more paper towels'. I heard 'order 400 paper towels'. They arrive Thursday." (99c)** — the more→400 mishearing is neat and the undercut is deadpan, but the twist is logistical and mild; the boss version's social damage is a sharper laugh.
- **C — "Sorry — you said 'set an alarm for six'. I set it for 6 p.m. Enjoy your nap." (77c)** — short and punchy, but "set alarm wrong" is a familiar smart-speaker joke shape; it loses on freshness.
